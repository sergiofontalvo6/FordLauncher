<p><b>FordLauncher</b> gives your car's Android head unit a new life: it replaces the generic factory menu with a modern home screen, without flashing anything or touching the system.</p>
<p>It is designed for the Chinese head units that claim "Android 6+" but actually run Android 4.x, where the Play Store no longer works and most apps won't install. It also works on modern units.</p>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Home screen with your vehicle (photo with automatic background removal), GPS speedometer, clock and weather.</li>
<li>Live map of your city with your position.</li>
<li>Android Auto and CarPlay buttons: opens your unit's projection app or Open Headunit.</li>
<li>Player with album art, title, artist and progress for Bluetooth or Android Auto audio.</li>
<li>Shortcuts to the factory Radio, Bluetooth, Camera, Browser and File apps (the reverse camera keeps working).</li>
<li>Dark, light or automatic theme and 10 accent colors.</li>
<li>Update notices from GitHub, with a QR code to download from your phone.</li>
</ul>
<p>Works on Android 4.1+. Free, no ads, open source (GPL-3.0). Interface in Spanish.</p>
